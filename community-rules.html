Feed rules

One page, five lines. The feed is for teachers, among colleagues.

  1. Share what is yours.Your own lessons, words and questions. Not someone else’s work, and not a course you are selling.
  2. No students.No names, photos, messages or notes about a student - not even a kind word. Class totals on a card are fine; people are not.
  3. Be useful and be kind.Answer the question that was asked. Disagree with the idea, not the person.
  4. No advertising.No promotions, referral links or repeated posts. The feed is not a shop window.
  5. Report what breaks these.Three reports hide a post until a person looks at it; nobody is judged by a machine, and nobody is told who reported.
