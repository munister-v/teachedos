# Halloween Pumpkin Pack

The 25 pumpkins (p01–p25.png) and the background tile (tile.png) were cut from
"Halloween Pumpkin Pack" by Katy Elizabeth:
https://katy-elizabeth.itch.io/halloween-pumpkin-pack

According to the pack page, it is free and may be used in commercial and free
projects, with no attribution required. Downloaded on 2026-09-27. The pieces
were cut from the pack's transparent PNG and scaled to 128 px.

They are used by the Pumpkin Night lesson theme (scripts/lesson-themes.js).
