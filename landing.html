Questions

Fair questions.

Do lessons happen inside TeachEd?

Your video call stays where it already is — for example Zoom. TeachEd is the live board, the materials, the homework and the memory around that call, so nothing about your routine has to change.

Is my students' data private?

Yes. The student dossier — notes, mistake patterns, progress — is visible only to you, the teacher. Students never see it, in any form. They see their hub, their words and their wins.

Can I use my own textbook?

Yes — the Book Page Importer turns a textbook page into interactive activities you can edit, chain into a lesson and assign. Your materials stay yours.

Which levels and languages?

TeachEd is built for language teaching across CEFR levels A1–C2. Every activity is matched to its level behind the scenes — rigor inside, lightness outside.

I have years of materials in other apps. Do I lose them?

No — and you do not start from zero. Import documents, PDFs and board exports; TeachEd rebuilds them as live, editable lessons with activities and homework — not dead pictures of lessons. Your archive moves with you.
