📖 Facts about Bread and Salt — Khlib-Sil

🤝 From stranger to family

The guest breaks off a piece of bread, dips it in salt, and eats. From that moment they are not a stranger in the house: the shared bread makes them almost a relative, with a relative's rights — and duties.

🚪 Where you will see it

Bread and salt still greet people today: at weddings, at village and city festivals, when a new school or bridge opens, and when honoured guests — even presidents and astronauts — arrive in Ukraine.

✋ Never refuse

Refusing bread and salt was a serious insult: it meant refusing friendship itself. And the loaf must not be turned upside down or left unfinished on the cloth — bread is treated with respect in every detail.

🌻 Why round?

The welcome loaf, palianytsia, is round and golden like the sun — a little sun you can hold in your hands, offered to the guest. On the rushnyk under it, red embroidery wishes life, love, and a long road home.
