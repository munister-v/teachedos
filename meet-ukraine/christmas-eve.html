📖 Facts about Christmas Eve — Sviata Vecheria

🍽️ Twelve dishes

The Christmas Eve supper has exactly 12 meatless dishes — for the twelve apostles. The table can hold borscht with mushroom vushka, varenyky with cabbage or potatoes, fish, pickled herring, beans, cabbage rolls with millet, and more — all Lenten, but festive.

⭐ The first star

Nobody eats until the first star appears. Children take turns watching the sky, and the moment it is spotted, the family says a prayer and sits down. For many children this waiting is the most magical part of the year.

🪑 A place for everyone

An extra place is always set at the table — for an unexpected traveller, and in memory of family members who are no longer alive. Food is left on the table overnight: tradition says the ancestors come to taste it.

🌾 The didukh stays

The didukh stands in the place of honour until the New Year or Epiphany. Then its grains were once sown in the field or fed to animals, so its blessing returned to the earth that gave it.
