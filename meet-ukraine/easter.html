📖 Facts about Easter — Velykden

🌅 The greeting

From Easter morning people greet each other with 'Khrystos voskres!' — 'Christ is risen!' The answer: 'Voyistynu voskres!' — 'Indeed He is risen!' For forty days after Easter, this replaces 'hello'.

🧺 The blessed breakfast

After the long Lent fast, the Easter breakfast begins with the blessed food from the basket — first a piece of paska and a blessed egg, shared by the whole family. Nothing from the basket is ever thrown away carelessly.

🥚 Navbytky — the egg game

Two players tap painted eggs together, tip to tip. Whose egg cracks, loses it. A champion egg that survives the whole village tournament is kept with pride — and clever players test eggs with a gentle tap of a tooth first.

🌿 Willow before palms

Ukraine has no palms, so willow — the first tree to wake in spring — took their place. Blessed willow was kept behind icons all year and used to gently drive the cattle to pasture for the first time, 'so they grow like the willow'.
