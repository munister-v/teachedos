📖 Facts about Ivan Kupala Night

☀️ The solstice night

Kupala celebrates the summer solstice — the sun at its strongest. It is a festival older than its Christian name: water, fire, herbs, and love fortunes all belong to one short, bright night when nobody wants to sleep.

💧 Water that heals

On Kupala, water was believed to gain special power: morning dew healed, and swimming before dawn washed away the year's troubles. Herbs gathered that night — St John's wort, wormwood, chamomile — were dried and kept as medicine for the whole year.

🕯️ Reading the river

A floating wreath was a question, and the river answered: floating far meant a journey or a wedding; catching on the bank meant another year at home. Young men waited downstream — catching a wreath meant catching its owner's attention.

🌿 A night for the brave

Midnight in the forest was not for the faint-hearted: legends filled Kupala night with spirits, treasures surfacing from the earth, and animals speaking. The stories made the night thrilling — and kept children safely by the fire.
