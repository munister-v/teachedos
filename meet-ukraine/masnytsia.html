📖 Facts about Masnytsia — Pancake Week

🤝 Forgiveness Sunday

The last day of Masnytsia is Forgiveness Sunday: people ask each other, honestly and by name, 'Forgive me' — and hear 'God will forgive, and I forgive.' You enter the fast with a light heart, owing nobody an apology.

👵 Mother-in-law's pancakes

One day of the week belonged to family visits — famously, sons-in-law came to their mothers-in-law for pancakes (teshchyni mlyntsi). Feeding the son-in-law well was diplomacy: a full son-in-law is a kind husband, the saying goes.

☀️ Eating the sun

Every pancake eaten was a small piece of sun — and the more suns eaten in the week, the warmer the spring would be, people joked while reaching for the next one. Thin, lacy, golden pancakes were a cook's pride and a family's forecast.

🔥 Winter, out!

Burning Marena was the finale: songs, jokes, and the whole village around the fire. The ashes were sometimes scattered on the fields — even winter, sent away politely, was asked to help the harvest on her way out.
