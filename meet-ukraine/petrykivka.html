📖 Facts about Petrykivka Painting

🏛️ UNESCO, 2013

In 2013 Petrykivka decorative painting entered UNESCO's Representative List of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity — the first Ukrainian tradition to receive this honour. It is named after one village, Petrykivka in Dnipropetrovsk region, where it was born.

✋ No sketch, no eraser

Masters paint 'from the heart' (vid sertsia): the composition grows on the surface stroke by stroke, with nothing drawn in advance. Children in the village learn from about five years old — first one petal, then a whole blooming panel.

🏠 Art that lived on houses

Originally the painting decorated the whitewashed walls and stoves of village houses: flowers greeted guests at the door and bloomed around the icons. A well-painted house was a family's pride — repainted fresh for big holidays.

🌺 Every flower means well

Kalyna means home and memory, sunflowers mean warmth and loyalty, poppies mean beauty and dreams, mallows mean a mother's love. A Petrykivka panel is a bouquet of good wishes you can read.
