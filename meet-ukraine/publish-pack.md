## Announcement post (Community, official account)

🇺🇦 **Meet Ukraine — a new Picture Studio series, made with love** 💛💙

Dear teachers, we made you something warm: thirteen hand-drawn interactive scenes about Ukrainian traditions — a cozy kitchen, Christmas Eve under the first star, Easter baskets, a wedding korovai, Kupala bonfires, golden harvest fields, and a whitewashed khata with mallows by the window.

Every picture is alive: tap an object to hear its Ukrainian name, read its story in simple or full English, and play — Find the Object, Say It Yourself, and our new ABC Quiz. Copy any scene to your board in one click and take your students to Ukraine without leaving the classroom.

Start with the Kitchen — and tell us in the comments which tradition your students loved most. 💛

## How to use in a lesson (for the post comments / pin)
1. Warm-up: open a scene in Labels mode as a poster — students name everything they already know.
2. Culture moment: read one fact together (Simple level for A2, Full for B1+).
3. Game time: ABC Quiz in pairs — loser has to say all the words aloud. 🔊
4. Homework with love: find one tradition that exists in your country too, and tell the class about it.
