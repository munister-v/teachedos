📖 Facts about St Nicholas Day — Den Mykolaia

🗓️ December 6

Ukraine celebrates St Nicholas Day on December 6. Children write him letters beforehand — not only asking, but also honestly reporting their year: what they learned, whom they helped, and yes, what mischief happened.

👞 Polished shoes

In western regions children clean their shoes the evening before and line them up by the window or door. A shiny shoe is a small exam: Mykolai, parents joke, does not put gifts into muddy boots.

💌 Letters and helpers

Every autumn 'Residences of Saint Nicholas' open in Ukrainian cities, where children bring letters and meet the saint in person. Behind the scenes, thousands of volunteers — Mykolai's helpers — make sure children in hospitals and shelters wake up to gifts too.

⭐ The first winter star

St Nicholas Day starts the winter holidays: from here the evenings belong to carols, rehearsals, and secrets wrapped in paper. Grandmothers say winter does not truly begin at the calendar — it begins when Mykolai knocks.
